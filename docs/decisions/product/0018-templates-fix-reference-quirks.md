## Decision

Templates fix three quirks in the PersonCarDog reference. Next is enabled from the start. Text is escaped. The page has a back link.

## Rationale

In the reference, Next stays disabled until autoplay starts, some text shows a literal escape sequence, and the diagram pages have no way back.
